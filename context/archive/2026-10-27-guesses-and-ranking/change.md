## Notes

Roadmap S-06 (guesses-and-ranking): a guest enters their guess of the final raise amount in PLN with grosze (>= 0) on their phone (personal link /g/<key>) and can change it until the organizer closes intake; the guest sees ONLY their own guess. The organizer can enter or correct a guess for any guest (proxy), sees who has not guessed yet, closes and reopens intake, enters the correct amount, and reads on their own device the ranking by absolute distance from the correct amount, from the LAST place, ties as equal places (competition ranking 1, 2, 2, 4) marked as ties. Guesses of others and the ranking never reach guest or TV routes. PRD refs: US-04, FR-017, FR-018, FR-019, FR-020, NFR-001.
