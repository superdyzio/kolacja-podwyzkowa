## Notes

Roadmap S-03 (manual-seating-on-tv): organizer seats guests on concrete seats, swaps two guests, pins a guest (incl. themself) to a seat and unpins, and publishes the arrangement; the read-only TV view /tv/<key> shows the venue plan with guest display names next to tables per the PUBLISHED arrangement and refreshes itself within ≤ 5 s after a publish (polling).
