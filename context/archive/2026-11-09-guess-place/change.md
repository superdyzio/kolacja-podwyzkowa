## Notes

User request (2026-11-06, the day the guest links go out): everyone still guesses the final amount (the main
contest), and next to it an OPTIONAL field "na którym miejscu będziesz". The organizer hands out a consolation prize
to those who do not win but hit their own final place.

Decisions (2026-11-06):

- Ties: a tied group covers every place it spans (1, 2, 2, 4 → the two at „2” occupy places 2–3), so a member of
  that group hits with a prediction of 2 or 3 (option A).
- Who is excluded from the consolation prize: not decided yet. Instead the ranking highlights the whole podium
  (gold, silver, bronze) and, in light green, everyone who hit their own place.
- A prediction is allowed only from 1 to the number of guests.
- Accepted defaults: a place needs an amount (an amount does not need a place); a prediction above the number of
  guesses is simply a miss; the organizer can enter a place for a guest; the guest can change it until intake
  closes; absent guests' guesses entered by the organizer count as in the ranking.
