## Notes

Roadmap S-07 (question-base-and-draw): the organizer adds, edits and deletes conversation questions with a category (kontrowersyjne / generyczne / refleksyjne) and bulk-pastes many at once; a starter base of ~30 Polish questions per category is ready (seeded at event creation + "Wczytaj bazę startową"); the organizer and each guest can draw a random question from a chosen category or from all, visible only to whoever drew it (no sync with TV or others).
