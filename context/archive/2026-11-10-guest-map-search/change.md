## Notes

— S-22 „Wyszukiwanie gościa na mapie sali” (PRD v2 FR-048, US-08). Sources: context/foundation/prd-v2.md, context/foundation/roadmap.md (### S-22), context/foundation/overnight-brief.md, shape-notes.md (Forward blocks). Everyone who sees the full venue map on a keyboard device (guest phone, client, staff incl. floor and per-event waiter, restaurant tablet) types part of a guest's name and the map highlights that guest's seat; the TV has no search. Search only over data the viewer already has (published snapshot for guests/tablet), no new data, routes, tokens or DTO fields.
