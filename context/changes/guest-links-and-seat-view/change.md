## Notes

Roadmap S-05 (guest-links-and-seat-view): the organizer prints cards with each guest's display name + personal QR code, copies each guest's personal link, turns guest access on/off and revokes a guest's link issuing a new one; a guest opening their personal link /g/<key> sees on the phone their display name, table (and room) and seat in the currently PUBLISHED arrangement, refreshed live within ≤ 5 s after a publish without any action (polling). PRD refs: US-03, FR-011, FR-012, FR-013, NFR-003, NFR-008, NFR-009.
