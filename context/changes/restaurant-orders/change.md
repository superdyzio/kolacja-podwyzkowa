## Notes

User request (2026-11-02): a new, separate link for the restaurant that presents the tables with the food ordered by
particular people (where to put what). The organizer sees and edits the full list in the panel, assigns each line to
a person or to a table (a loose line placed on the table), sees prices, the total, quantities and ticks what was
delivered. The restaurant sees only the list for its prep and the placement for serving.

Decisions (AskUserQuestion, 2026-11-02): free list with dish autocomplete and last-price prefill; the restaurant also
sees what was served (no prices); persons are placed by the PUBLISHED seating; the link is always on with
„Wymień link”.
